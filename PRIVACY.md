# EagleCore Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
EagleCore is a Google Chrome extension designed to help authorized employees manage orders and perform supported workflows on company WMS and related systems.

## 2. Data We Handle
Depending on the features used, EagleCore may access or store:
- WMS login credentials entered by the employee.
- Employee ID and extension settings.
- Order details and product information needed for order management.
- Relevant company website content and chat history needed to process and verify transfer workflows.
- Notification scripts and branch mappings configured by the employee.

## 3. How Data Is Used
This information is used only to provide the extension's order filtering, sorting, transfer, notification, administrative note, and settings features.

## 4. Storage
EagleCore uses Chrome storage mechanisms. Some settings and credentials may be stored in Chrome Storage Sync, while other information may be stored locally in Chrome Storage Local. The extension does not provide additional encryption for credentials stored through these mechanisms.

## 5. Data Sharing
EagleCore is not intended to sell user data or use it for advertising. Data may be processed within the company's systems as necessary to perform the employee's requested workflows. Any additional sharing with third parties must be accurately disclosed by the extension operator.

## 6. Security and Retention
Users should protect their devices and WMS accounts and should not share login credentials. Data retained by company systems is subject to the company's applicable policies. Users can remove the extension and its locally stored data through Chrome settings; synchronized or company-system data may require separate deletion.

## 7. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the extension administrator or the company responsible for operating EagleCore.

## 8. Changes
This policy may be updated if the extension's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL.
